Investment thesis

We prioritize durability: resilient cash flows, strong counterparties, transparent governance, and downside-protected structures.

Capital preservation first

We underwrite conservatively and avoid stories that require perfect execution to work.

Owner-operator alignment

We prefer founders and operators who think in decades, not quarters.

Simple structures

Clean cap tables, clear rights, and straightforward decision-making beat complexity.

Focus areas

Example categories—edit to match your actual mandate. Keep it tight; family office pages should not read like a menu.

Real assets

Core real estate, logistics, essential infrastructure, and inflation-hedged assets.

  • Prime residential & mixed-use
  • Industrial & last-mile logistics
  • Yielding assets with clear value-add

Private companies

Cash-flowing businesses with defensible moats and rational capital allocation.

  • Business services
  • Specialty finance (where regulated + prudent)
  • Tech-enabled but fundamentals-led

Structured opportunities

Situations where terms and structure matter more than hype.

  • Preferred equity / mezzanine
  • Revenue-share / royalties
  • Asset-backed lending

Co-investments

Selective partnerships with proven operators and transparent reporting.

  • Aligned GP incentives
  • Clear reporting cadence
  • Defined liquidity pathways

How we work

Fast, disciplined, and quiet. If it’s not a fit, we say so early.

1) Filter

Mandate check, basic diligence, and alignment review (48–72 hours when data is clean).

2) Underwrite

Downside case, unit economics, counterparties, and legal structure—no shortcuts.

3) Execute

Simple term sheets, clear conditions, and decisive execution once conviction is earned.

Contact

Share a brief overview and we’ll respond if aligned. For sensitive materials, request a secure channel.

        `Name: ${data.name}\nEmail: ${data.email}\nCompany/Project: ${data.company || "-"}\nLocation: ${data.location || "-"}\n\nMessage:\n${data.message}\n`

      );


      // TODO: Replace with your target email address:

      const to = "info@yourdomain.com";
